## Full description (max 4000)

```
Block puts Bitcoin on your home screen. Clean, readable widgets in a minimal blockclock style show the numbers you check every day — at a glance, without opening an app.

WIDGETS
• Bitcoin metrics — price, sats per dollar, block height, fees, hash rate, difficulty, halving countdown, Lightning capacity, Strategy (MSTR) holdings and more. Tap to cycle; choose which metrics and their order.
• Price chart — BTC price with a 24-hour or 7-day sparkline. Tap to switch.
• Halving countdown — progress, days or blocks to the next halving.
• Bitcoin quotes — words from Satoshi Nakamoto, Hal Finney and the cypherpunks.
Every widget is resizable and stays sharp at any size.

IN THE APP
• Dashboard with a large panel for the number you care about most and an interactive 24H / 7D price chart.
• 24-hour change on prices and sats.
• Your stack: enter how much bitcoin you hold and see what it is worth. It never leaves your device.
• Alerts: get notified when BTC crosses a price, when fees drop low, a day before the halving and before each difficulty adjustment.
• USD or THB, with your choice of price source (Coinbase or Kraken; converted or Bitkub for THB).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings and holdings stay on your phone. Market data comes straight from public sources such as Coinbase and mempool.space.

Block shows information only. It is not a wallet or an exchange and does not give financial advice.
```
