# Blockface Community Rules

Blockface is a window onto Nostr, an open network that no company runs. Blockface doesn't host posts, but these rules apply to everyone using the app. The same text is shown in the app (Settings › Nostr › Community rules, and at sign-up).

## Not allowed

- **Nothing illegal.** No content that is illegal where you or your readers are, including child sexual abuse material, which is never tolerated.
- **No harassment or hate.** Don't threaten, harass, bully or dox anyone, and don't attack people for who they are.
- **No spam or scams.** No repetitive or automated posting, fake giveaways, phishing, or posts that trick people out of money or keys.
- **No impersonation.** Don't pretend to be another person or organisation in a way that misleads.
- **Label sensitive content.** Mark adult or graphic content as such, and keep it out of replies to people who didn't ask for it.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post other people's private information or intimate images without their consent.

## Your tools

- **Mute** hides an account everywhere in Blockface (⋯ on a post; Unmute on their profile).
- **Report** (⋯ › Report on a post) publishes a NIP-56 report to your relays so relays and other apps can act on it.
- **Contact:** for anything urgent or illegal, open an issue at <https://github.com/phirapath21/btcwidget/issues>, or contact the relay that hosts the post.

Posts on Nostr are public and permanent: relays may keep copies even after you delete. Breaking these rules can get you reported, muted by others, and refused by relays.
